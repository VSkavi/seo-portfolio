How I Work

My SEO project workflow

  1. 01

    Keyword research

    Identify high-intent search terms using Google Keyword Planner.

  2. 02

    Competitor analysis

    Review competing content and record findings in a Google Sheet.

  3. 03

    On-site SEO

    Optimize titles, meta descriptions, headings, and images; build clean, structured URLs.

  4. 04

    Technical SEO

    Check mobile responsiveness, page loading speed, and overall site performance.

  5. 05

    Submit & audit

    Upload to Google Search Console, then generate a comprehensive report through a full site audit.
